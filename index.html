Why It Matters

Water is life, but it is not infinite.

Global Crisis

Over 2 billion people live in countries experiencing high water stress. Conserving water ensures equity for all.

Ecosystem Balance

Freshwater ecosystems support 10% of all known species. Saving water protects biodiversity and natural habitats.

Economic Savings

Reducing water waste lowers utility bills for families and reduces energy costs used for water treatment.
