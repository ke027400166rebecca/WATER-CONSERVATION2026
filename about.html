Our Story

Empowering Kenyan communities, one drop at a time.

Who We Are

AquaGuard Kenya began in our school corridors with a simple realization: water scarcity affects every corner of our nation, from urban Nairobi to rural villages. Founded in 2024 by a group of passionate Kenyan students, our mission is to bridge the gap between awareness and action in our communities.

We believe that the youth of Kenya hold the key to sustainable change. By providing accessible tools, educational resources, and community-driven projects, we empower students and families to become stewards of our precious water resources. From the shores of Lake Victoria to the arid regions of Turkana, every drop matters.

Our Core Values

Our Mission

To educate and equip Kenyan communities with the knowledge and tools necessary to reduce water waste by 30% within the next decade, starting with our schools.

Our Vision

A Kenya where every child has access to clean water, ecosystems thrive from Mount Kenya to the coast, and no community faces water scarcity.

Our Commitment

We commit to community-led solutions, cultural sensitivity, and empowering the next generation of Kenyan environmental leaders.

Our Journey

Milestones in our movement

January 2024

Project Inception

AquaGuard Kenya was founded by 5 students in Nairobi, starting with local water audits in our school.

June 2024

First Community Drive

Launched our first water conservation campaign in Kibera, reaching over 200 families with education and rainwater harvesting tips.

March 2025

National Expansion

Partnered with 15 schools across 5 counties, launching our interactive website and calculator to track water savings.

Present Day

Growing Movement

Continuing to expand our reach, working with local chiefs and educators to create lasting change in water conservation practices.
