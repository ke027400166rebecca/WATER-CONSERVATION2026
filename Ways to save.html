Practical Ways to Save Water

Simple actions you can take today at home, school, and in the community.

Where Can You Save?

Small changes in your daily routine make a huge difference.

At Home

  • Turn off the tap while brushing teeth (saves 6L/min).
  • Fix leaking taps immediately.
  • Take shorter showers (5 minutes or less).
  • Use a bucket instead of a hose to wash cars.
  • Only run washing machines with full loads.

At School

  • Report broken pipes to the administration.
  • Don't play with water taps during break time.
  • Start a "Water Monitor" club in your class.
  • Use leftover water from bottles to water school plants.
  • Educate younger students about conservation.

In Community

  • Install rainwater harvesting tanks.
  • Protect water sources from pollution.
  • Teach neighbors about fixing leaks.
  • Participate in local river cleanup drives.
  • Use drip irrigation for kitchen gardens.

Did You Know?

A dripping tap can waste over 20 liters of water a day. That's enough for 40 cups of tea!

The Power of Rainwater Harvesting

In Kenya, rain is a gift we cannot waste. Collecting rainwater from roofs is one of the most effective ways to ensure water security during dry seasons.

  • Cost Effective: Reduces water bills significantly.
  • Clean Water: Provides soft water ideal for washing and gardening.
  • Flood Control: Reduces runoff and soil erosion during heavy rains.
