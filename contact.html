Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions

How can my school join the AquaGuard program?

We love working with schools! Simply fill out the contact form above selecting "School Partnership" as the subject, and our team will reach out to schedule a visit.

Do you offer volunteering opportunities?

Yes! We welcome student volunteers for community cleanups, awareness campaigns, and educational workshops. Contact us to learn about upcoming events.

How can I report a water leak in my community?

You can report leaks directly through our contact form by selecting "Report Water Issue". Please include the location and details of the leak so we can forward it to the relevant authorities.

Is the water calculator accurate for all regions in Kenya?

The calculator provides estimates based on average usage. Actual consumption may vary depending on your specific fixtures and habits, but it's a great tool for awareness!
